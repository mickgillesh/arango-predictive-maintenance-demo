# SB-IAE-V2500-A5-72-0103 — HPC Stage 5-7 Blade Platform Repair and Coating Restoration

**Document Type:** Service Bulletin  
**Recommended By:** IAE International Aero Engines  
**Effective Date:** 2024-04-22  
**Affected Engine Models:** V2527-A5  
**Affected Subsystems:** HPC  
**Recommended Interval:** At every HPC module removal where stage 5-7 blades are within life limits but show platform wear  
**Supersedes:** None  
**Related Documents:** AD-EASA-2023-1423, AMM-V2500-72-00-00-200-802, AMM-GEN-77-00-00-ECTM-001

## Summary

Approved plasma spray repair procedure for HPC stage 5-7 blade platform erosion on V2527-A5 engines, allowing blades with erosion between 0.15-0.40 mm depth to be repaired rather than scrapped — saving approximately USD 322,000 per engine visit where applicable.

## Applicability

| Engine Model | Module | Blade Stages | Blade Part Number Series |
|---|---|---|---|
| V2527-A5 | HPC | 5, 6, 7 | 2A3xxx series, all dash numbers |
| V2527E-A5 (SelectOne) | HPC | 5, 6, 7 | 2A5xxx series |

The repair is applicable at shop visit only. It cannot be performed on wing.

## Reason

The overhaul manual previously required every stage 5-7 blade with any platform erosion to be scrapped. Operator data show that 58% of stage 5-7 blades removed at HPC shop visits have platform erosion, but only 9% exceed 0.40 mm. The blanket scrap criterion therefore generated large, avoidable material costs and added to the long lead time for replacement HPC blades. The approved repair restores the platform to drawing dimensions with a plasma-sprayed coating that has been qualified for full blade life.

## Recommended Actions

1. Assess HPC stage 5-7 blade platform surfaces for erosion and corrosion per IAE CMM 72-33-00.
2. Blades with platform erosion between 0.15 mm and 0.40 mm depth may be repaired using approved plasma spray coating application per IAE Approved Repair AR-72-33-019.
3. Blades with erosion exceeding 0.40 mm must be removed from service.
4. Apply corrosion protection coating to all repaired platforms per AR-72-33-019 Section 5.
5. Re-weigh and re-moment blades after repair; verify blade set remains within moment-weight group tolerances.
6. Combine the assessment with the stage 6-8 seal inspection required by AD-EASA-2023-1423, which also needs the blades removed from the drum.
7. Use the platform erosion estimates recorded during in-situ borescope inspections (AMM-V2500-72-00-00-200-802) to plan repair capacity and replacement blade provisioning before the shop visit.

## Repair Limits

| Platform Condition | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Erosion less than 0.15 mm, no corrosion | Serviceable — no repair |
| Erosion 0.15-0.40 mm | Repair per AR-72-33-019 |
| Erosion greater than 0.40 mm | Scrap |
| Corrosion pitting, depth less than 0.10 mm | Blend and repair per AR-72-33-019 |
| Corrosion pitting, depth 0.10 mm or more | Scrap |
| Cracks at platform edge or fillet | Scrap |
| Blade previously repaired twice | Scrap (maximum two repairs per blade) |

## Background and Technical Details

IAE International Aero Engines issues this Service Bulletin to provide guidance on the repair and restoration of HPC stage 5-7 blade platforms for V2527-A5 engines, allowing serviceable blades with platform erosion to be returned to service after approved repair rather than scrapped. The approved repair procedure described in IAE AR-72-33-019 was developed in response to operator feedback that the existing overhaul manual criterion — reject all blades with any platform erosion — was unnecessarily conservative and was generating premature blade scrap.

The HPC stage 5-7 blades are exposed to the compressor gas path and experience erosion on the platform surfaces from particulate matter entrained in the compressed airflow. The platform erosion does not directly affect the aerodynamic performance of the blade since the platform is outside the gas path boundary; however, erosion can reduce the effective seating area of the blade in the disc slot and potentially affect the fretting behaviour of the blade-disc interface under high centrifugal loading. IAE's materials engineering team has validated that platform erosion up to 0.40 mm depth can be repaired to restore the full structural integrity of the platform interface, provided the repair is performed by a qualified facility using the approved plasma spray process.

The qualification programme included metallographic examination of 120 repaired blades, spin-pit testing to 115% of maximum certified speed, and a 3,000-cycle endurance test on a V2527-A5 development engine with a full repaired stage 6. No coating loss, no change in blade natural frequency beyond 1%, and no fretting distress at the dovetail were found. On this basis IAE has approved two repairs per blade over its life.

From an operator's point of view, the repair also shortens shop visit turn time. Replacement HPC blades for the V2527-A5 have had lead times of 90 to 120 days during periods of high shop visit demand. A shop visit that can repair most stage 5-7 blades depends far less on new blade availability. Operators with V2527-A5 engines flagged for HPC deterioration in their trend monitoring programme should collect the platform erosion estimates from in-situ borescope inspections, so that the shop can plan repair capacity and order only the blades that will exceed the 0.40 mm limit.

## Engine Health Monitoring Indicators

Platform erosion itself does not produce a trend signature, because the platform is outside the gas path. However, the particulate exposure that erodes platforms also erodes airfoils and wears seals. Engines showing T30, Nc and htBleed drift (see AMM-GEN-77-00-00-ECTM-001) are likely to have platform erosion in the repairable range at their next HPC shop visit.

## Definitions

- **Platform:** The flat section of a compressor blade between the airfoil and the dovetail that forms the inner gas path wall.
- **Plasma spray:** A coating process that melts powder in a plasma jet and deposits it on a surface.
- **Moment-weight:** The product of blade mass and radial centre of gravity, used to balance a stage.
