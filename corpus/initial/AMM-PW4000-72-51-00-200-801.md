## Task Summary

This task gives the procedure to measure PW4077D HPT stage 1 blade tip clearance at four positions with the capacitance probe fixture, to inspect the shroud segments and blade tips with a borescope, and to take a gas path debris sample. It is the accomplishment procedure for AD-FAA-2023-1889.

## Job Set-Up

WARNING: TAG OUT THE ENGINE AND OPEN THE EEC AND IGNITION CIRCUIT BREAKERS.

WARNING: HPT ACCESS IS SUBJECT TO FOD CONTROL. START A TOOL COUNT LOG. LOSS OF A TOOL INTO THE GAS PATH MUST BE REPORTED TO THE FAA WITHIN 24 HOURS.

CAUTION: THE CASE TEMPERATURE MUST BE BELOW 50°C FOR THE CAPACITANCE PROBE CALIBRATION TO BE VALID.

1. Open fan cowls and thrust reverser halves.
2. Install the N2 cranking adaptor.
3. Remove the four probe boss plugs on the HPT case.
4. Calibrate the capacitance probe on the reference standard in kit 1C9869.

## Procedure

1. Install the capacitance probe in the 0° boss. Turn N2 slowly through one full revolution and record the minimum clearance of every blade passing the probe.
2. Repeat at 90°, 180° and 270°.
3. Calculate the average clearance and the asymmetry (maximum position average minus minimum position average).
4. Remove the probe. Insert the videoscope through the 180° boss and inspect the shroud segments for wear grooves, cracks, coating loss and segment step (misalignment between adjacent segments).
5. Inspect blade tips for rub marks, cracks, burn-back and tip cap condition.
6. Take the gas path debris sample per SL-72-218 from the HPT case drain.
7. Compare with the limits.

## Inspection Limits

| Measurement | Serviceable | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Average clearance | 0.45 mm or more | Less than 0.45 mm |
| Asymmetry | 0.25 mm or less | Greater than 0.25 mm |
| Shroud wear groove depth | 0.3 mm or less | Greater than 0.3 mm (replace segments) |
| Segment step | 0.2 mm or less | Greater than 0.2 mm |
| Blade tip crack | None | Any |
| Blade tip burn-back | Up to 0.5 mm | Greater than 0.5 mm |

## Fault Isolation Notes

| Trend Observation (AMM-GEN-77-00-00-ECTM-001) | Likely Finding | Action |
|---|---|---|
| W31 rising, EGT rising, T30 stable | Shroud wear, clearance growth | This task |
| N2 vibration step change | Tip rub | This task before next flight |
| EGT rising with fuel flow ratio (phi) rising | Combustor distress affecting HPT | Combustor inspection too |
| EGT rising with T30 and Nc rising | HPC deterioration | HPC borescope (AMM-PW4000-72-00-00-200-801) |

## Service Experience

Since AD-FAA-2023-1889 became effective, the operator has measured all PW4077D engines at least once. Results:

| Result | Share of Engines | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Average ≥ 0.45 mm and asymmetry ≤ 0.25 mm | 71% | None |
| Asymmetry 0.20-0.25 mm (close to limit) | 15% | Re-measure in 500 cycles |
| Asymmetry > 0.25 mm | 8% | HPT shroud segment set replaced at shop |
| Average < 0.45 mm | 6% | HPT shroud segment set replaced; blade tips inspected |

Asymmetric wear was concentrated in the lower sector (150°-210°), where the combustor exit temperature pattern is hottest on this engine. Two engines with asymmetry above limit also had coked fuel nozzles upstream of the worn sector, found during AMM-PW4000-72-41-00-200-801. HPT cooling bleed (W31) had risen by more than 2% on five of the eight engines that were rejected, confirming W31 as a useful early indicator. HPT shroud segment sets and HPT nozzle guide vanes are long-lead items; the HPT nozzle guide vane has been out of stock at times.

## Close-Up

1. Complete the tool count.
2. Install the probe boss plugs with new gaskets; torque per AMM 72-51-00.
3. Remove the N2 cranking adaptor.
4. Close the thrust reverser halves and fan cowls.

## Records

Record AD-FAA-2023-1889 compliance; the four position averages, overall average and asymmetry; shroud and tip findings; debris sample reference; and the next due date.

## Background

The PW4077D HPT stage 1 shroud is made of segments that form a ring around the blade tips. Each segment is cooled and coated with an abradable layer. In long-haul operation the shroud runs at steady temperatures and wears slowly and evenly. In medium-haul high-cycle operation, repeated thrust changes create circumferential temperature differences and the segments wear unevenly. AD-FAA-2023-1889 requires the four-position measurement so that this asymmetry is found before it allows a blade rub. Measuring at four positions rather than one is the key point: an average clearance can look acceptable while one sector is close to contact.
