# AD-EASA-2024-0756 — Fan Blade Platform Fatigue Crack Inspection

**Document Type:** Airworthiness Directive  
**Issuer:** European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA)  
**Effective Date:** 2024-04-03  
**Affected Engine Models:** Trent 700, GE90-115B  
**Affected Subsystems:** fan  
**Compliance Interval:** Every 1,000 cycles from effective date  
**Supersedes:** None  
**Related Documents:** SB-RR-TRENT700-72-0289

## Summary

Mandatory fluorescent penetrant inspection of fan blade platform undersides on Trent 700 and GE90-115B engines following detection of fatigue cracking at the platform-to-dovetail fillet radius driven by centrifugal bending loads.

## Applicability

| Engine Model | Aircraft | Fan Blade Type |
|---|---|---|
| Trent 772B-60, 772C-60, 768-60 | Airbus A330 | Wide-chord hollow titanium |
| GE90-115B | Boeing 777-300ER, 777-200LR | Composite with titanium leading edge and root |

This AD does not apply to the CFM56, V2500 or PW4000 families.

## Unsafe Condition

Fatigue cracks at the fan blade platform-to-dovetail fillet radius, if not detected, can propagate and lead to release of a platform section or of the blade, with risk of fan case penetration and damage to the aircraft.

## Compliance

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| All engines | Initial inspection within 500 cycles after the effective date; repeat every 1,000 cycles |
| After water ingestion, bird strike or operation in salted/contaminated environment | Unscheduled inspection within 25 cycles of the event |

## Mandatory Actions

1. Remove each fan blade and inspect the platform underside using fluorescent penetrant inspection per manufacturer's NDT manual.
2. Measure platform dovetail neck width using calibrated micrometer; reject if below minimum limit in Table 1 of this AD.
3. Inspect platform-to-blade fillet radius for stress corrosion cracking; any crack indication is cause for immediate rejection.
4. Reinstall with new corrosion inhibiting compound (CIC) applied to dovetail surfaces per AMM 72-21-00.

## Table 1 — Dovetail Neck Width Limits

| Engine | Minimum Neck Width |
|---|---|
| Trent 700 | 41.20 mm |
| GE90-115B | 47.85 mm |

## Special Tools Required

- Calibrated outside micrometer, 0-50 mm range, 0.001 mm resolution
- UV lamp, 365 nm wavelength, minimum 1000 μW/cm² intensity
- Fluorescent penetrant kit, Zyglo ZL-56 or equivalent

## Safety Warnings

CAUTION: Platform fatigue cracks may not be visible to the unaided eye. Fluorescent penetrant inspection must be performed in a darkened area with UV lamp at the specified intensity. Inspectors must allow minimum 20 minutes dark adaptation before beginning inspection. Do not use the inspected surface as a structural indicator — only FPI results are acceptable.

## Background and Technical Details

EASA issues this Airworthiness Directive following the detection of platform fatigue cracks on fan blades from both Rolls-Royce Trent 700 and GE Aviation GE90-115B high-bypass turbofan engines. The cracks originate at the blade platform-to-dovetail fillet radius, driven by centrifugal bending loads on the platform during high-power operation. The platform experiences combined centrifugal tensile loading from its own mass and bending loads transmitted from the blade root dovetail. At high fan speeds, these combined loads can produce stress concentrations at the fillet radius that exceed the material endurance limit for titanium Ti-6Al-4V after approximately 800-1,200 cycles, depending on platform geometry and the specific local stress concentration factor.

Both the Trent 700 and GE90-115B use wide-chord fan blade designs with composite blade structures bonded to titanium leading edges and root systems. The platform section is manufactured from titanium forgings. EASA investigation found that a manufacturing dimensional variation within drawing tolerance, specifically a fillet radius at the lower end of the tolerance band, produces platform sections with fatigue lives approximately 25% shorter than the design nominal. Engines with blades from three specific production batches — identified in the accompanying Table 2 — are most susceptible, but given the difficulty of reliably identifying production batch from in-service markings, the inspection requirement applies to all engines.

The 1,000-cycle inspection interval provides a minimum safety factor of 2 against crack propagation to a critical length based on fracture mechanics analysis using the measured crack growth rates. Operators should note that water ingestion events, bird strikes, or ground operations in salted or contaminated environments may accelerate corrosion at the fillet radius and require reduced intervals — operators experiencing any of these events should perform an unscheduled inspection within 25 cycles of the event.

The root-fillet fretting and fatigue mechanism has parallels in the Trent 700 HPT disc dovetail fretting addressed by SB-RR-TRENT700-72-0289. Operators of Trent 700 engines may combine records management for both programmes.

## Definitions

- **Platform:** The part of the fan blade between airfoil and dovetail that forms the inner flow path.
- **Fillet radius:** The curved transition between two surfaces, here between platform and dovetail.
- **CIC:** Corrosion inhibiting compound.
