## Task Summary

This task gives the borescope inspection of the eleven-stage PW4077D high pressure compressor and the in-situ assessment of the stage 6-8 interstage labyrinth seals allowed by AD-EASA-2023-1423. It is the standard response to an HPC trend alert on the PW4077D.

## Job Set-Up

WARNING: TAG OUT THE ENGINE; OPEN THE EEC AND IGNITION CIRCUIT BREAKERS.

CAUTION: LET THE ENGINE COOL FOR 45 MINUTES.

1. Open fan cowls and thrust reverser halves.
2. Install the N2 cranking adaptor.
3. Calibrate the stereo measurement tip on calibration block PWA 85240; the error must be within ±0.1 mm.

## Procedure

1. Inspect HPC blades through each port while turning N2: leading edge erosion, tip curl, nicks, cracks, coating loss and fouling deposits.
2. On the rear stages, look for rub marks on blade tips and for evidence of seal rub debris.
3. Seal assessment: through the stage 6, 7 and 8 ports, view the interstage labyrinth seals. At eight positions per stage, measure knife edge height and radial clearance to the land. Note abradable coating loss and any backing ring cracks.
4. Compare with the limits below. If measurement accuracy cannot be assured at any position, the AD requires module removal.
5. If no defects are found and the engine is due a wash, wash after the inspection.

## Inspection Limits

| Feature | Serviceable | Reject |
|---|---|---|
| Knife edge height, stages 6-8 | 1.2 mm or more | Less than 1.2 mm |
| Seal land clearance, stages 6-8 | Up to 1.8 mm | Greater than 1.8 mm |
| Backing ring crack | None | Any |
| Blade leading edge erosion | Up to 1.0 mm | Greater than 1.0 mm |
| Blade tip curl | Up to 0.8 mm | Greater than 0.8 mm |
| Blade crack | None | Any |

## Fault Isolation Notes

| Trend Observation (AMM-GEN-77-00-00-ECTM-001) | Likely Finding |
|---|---|
| T30 rising, Ps30 falling at constant N2, htBleed rising, no wash recovery | Seal clearance growth |
| T30 and Nc rising, EGT margin falling, partial wash recovery | Fouling plus erosion |
| T24 also rising | LPC contribution — check VSV rigging (AMM-PW4000-72-31-00-200-801) |
| Fuel flow ratio (phi) rising | Combustor contribution (AMM-PW4000-72-41-00-200-801) |

In the PW4077D fleet, the HPC is the most frequent driver subsystem for engines in the warning and critical risk buckets, often with the fan or the combustor as secondary drivers. When HPC and fan drift together, check for fan blade leading edge erosion as well, because the same ingestion environment affects both.

## Service Experience

Over three years, the operator performed 85 PW4077D HPC borescope inspections, most of them raised by trend monitoring. Findings:

| Finding | Share | Typical Driver Sensors |
|---|---|---|
| Fouling only, recovered by wash | 31% | T30, Ps30 |
| Leading edge erosion within limits | 28% | T30, Nc, NRc |
| Seal clearance 1.5-1.8 mm (close to limit) | 18% | T30, Ps30, htBleed |
| Seal clearance above 1.8 mm — module removal | 7% | htBleed, Ps30 |
| Blade damage (FOD) | 6% | Nc, N2 vibration |
| No HPC finding — cause elsewhere | 10% | phi (combustor) or T24 (LPC) |

The 10% of inspections with no HPC finding show why the fault isolation matrix in AMM-GEN-77-00-00-ECTM-001 matters: when fuel flow ratio or LPC exit temperature also drift, the HPC may be reacting to a problem upstream or downstream. Seal clearance growth was concentrated in engines between 3,000 and 4,000 cycles since the last HPC shop visit, consistent with the 4,000-cycle limit in AD-EASA-2023-1423.

Replacement HPC interstage seals and HPC disks have had long lead times. When in-situ measurements show several positions above 1.5 mm, plan the shop visit and order the HPC interstage seal set in advance rather than waiting for the limit to be reached.

## Close-Up

1. Remove the videoscope; check the tip.
2. Install plugs with new gaskets; torque per AMM 72-00-00.
3. Remove the N2 cranking adaptor.
4. Close thrust reverser halves and fan cowls.

## Records

Record AD-EASA-2023-1423 compliance (in-situ method), all seal measurements, calibration result, blade findings per stage and next due date.

## Background

The PW4077D HPC has eleven stages and a pressure ratio of about 10:1. Its rear stages operate at high temperatures and very small blade heights; leakage through the stage 6-8 interstage seals has a disproportionate effect on efficiency. The seals wear as the engine cycles between take-off and idle and the rotor and case grow at different rates. AD-EASA-2023-1423 was issued after a fleet survey found 12% of seals beyond 1.8 mm clearance, with a 2.1% fuel burn penalty. The in-situ option avoids unscheduled module removals, but only if the measurement can be trusted; when in doubt, remove the module.
