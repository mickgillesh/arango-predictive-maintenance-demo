## Task Summary

This task gives the borescope inspection of the V2527-A5 HPT stage 1 nozzle guide vanes (NGVs) and stage 1 blades for thermal barrier coating (TBC) loss, oxidation, burns, cracks and tip distress. It supports the interim inspection recommended by SB-IAE-V2500-A5-72-0087 until the upgraded blade set is installed.

## Job Set-Up

WARNING: TAG OUT THE ENGINE. OPEN THE FADEC AND IGNITION CIRCUIT BREAKERS. DISCONNECT THE IGNITION LEADS IF USING THE IGNITER PORTS.

CAUTION: THE HPT STAYS HOT FOR UP TO 90 MINUTES. HOT COMPONENTS DAMAGE BORESCOPE OPTICS AND CAN CAUSE BURNS.

CAUTION: HPT COMPONENTS ARE SUBJECT TO FOREIGN OBJECT DAMAGE (FOD) CONTROL. ACCOUNT FOR ALL TOOLS BEFORE AND AFTER THE INSPECTION.

1. Open the C-ducts.
2. Install the HP rotor turning tool.
3. Remove HPT borescope plugs H1 and H2.

## Procedure

1. NGVs: insert the videoscope through an igniter port and inspect the stage 1 NGV leading edges, pressure sides and platforms. Record burns, cracks, coating loss and trailing edge erosion.
2. Blades: insert the guide tube and videoscope through port H1. Turn the HP rotor and inspect each of the 66 blades:
   - leading edge — TBC loss, oxidation, burns;
   - pressure and suction sides — coating loss area using reference chart RC-72-51-01;
   - tip — oxidation, burn-back, cracks, tip cap loss;
   - cooling holes — blockage.
3. Repeat through port H2 to view the trailing edges and platforms.
4. Estimate the percentage TBC loss on the worst blade and on the stage average.
5. Look for patterns: TBC loss concentrated on blades that pass a particular NGV sector points to a combustor hot streak; do AMM-V2500-72-41-00-200-801.

## Inspection Limits

| Feature | Serviceable | Re-inspect in 250 Cycles | Reject (Remove HPT) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blade TBC loss, leading edge | Up to 10% of area | 10-25% | Greater than 25%, or base metal oxidation |
| Blade tip burn-back | Up to 0.5 mm | 0.5-1.0 mm | Greater than 1.0 mm |
| Blade crack | None | Not applicable | Any |
| Cooling holes blocked | Up to 2 holes per blade | 3-5 holes | More than 5 holes or adjacent holes |
| NGV leading edge burn | Up to 3 mm | 3-6 mm | Greater than 6 mm or through-hole |
| NGV crack | Up to 10 mm, not at platform | 10-15 mm | Greater than 15 mm or at platform |

## Fault Isolation Notes

| Trend Observation (AMM-GEN-77-00-00-ECTM-001) | Likely Finding | Action |
|---|---|---|
| W31 rising, EGT rising, T30 stable | HPT blade TBC loss | This task |
| EGT rising, T30 rising, N2 rising | HPC deterioration loading the HPT | HPC borescope first (AMM-V2500-72-00-00-200-802) |
| EGT spread widening, sector-specific HPT burns | Combustor hot streak | Combustor inspection |
| Step change in N2 vibration | Blade tip loss | This task, then shop visit |

In the V2527-A5 fleet, HPT is a less common driver subsystem than HPC or combustor, but when it appears it is usually alongside HPC drift, and the two often share a root cause in high-cycle operation.

## Close-Up

1. Remove the videoscope and guide tube; check the tip.
2. Complete the tool count.
3. Install the plugs with new seals; torque per AMM 72-00-00.
4. Remove the HP rotor turning tool.
5. Reconnect the ignition leads if disconnected.
6. Close the C-ducts.

## Records

Record cycles on the current blade set, whether SB-IAE-V2500-A5-72-0087 is incorporated, the TBC loss estimate (worst blade and average), tip condition, NGV condition, images, and the next due inspection. Use the trend of TBC loss across inspections to plan the HPT module removal and the blade set upgrade.

## Background

The HPT stage 1 blades are the hottest rotating parts in the engine. They are single-crystal nickel alloy castings with internal cooling passages and film cooling holes, protected by a ceramic thermal barrier coating. The TBC can lower the blade metal temperature by over 100°C. When it spalls, the exposed metal oxidises quickly, starting at the leading edge and the tip, where the heat load is greatest.

High-cycle operation is hard on TBCs: each start and shutdown expands and contracts the ceramic and the metal at different rates, and the original plasma-sprayed coating cracks after enough cycles. The EB-PVD coating in SB-IAE-V2500-A5-72-0087 has a columnar structure that tolerates this strain much better. Until it is installed, this inspection lets the operator track coating loss and remove the HPT module at a planned time rather than after an EGT exceedance.
