# SB-GE-GE90-72-0288 — LPT Stage 5-7 Airfoil Trailing Edge Modification for Efficiency Improvement

**Document Type:** Service Bulletin  
**Recommended By:** GE Aviation  
**Effective Date:** 2023-12-20  
**Affected Engine Models:** GE90-115B  
**Affected Subsystems:** LPT  
**Recommended Interval:** At next LPT module removal  
**Supersedes:** None  
**Related Documents:** SB-GE-GE90-72-0312

## Summary

Aerodynamically improved LPT Stage 5-7 blade design for GE90-115B engines with 3% chord extension and optimised camber, eliminating descent-condition flow separation and delivering 0.09% SFC improvement — approximately USD 180,000 fuel saving per aircraft per year.

## Applicability

| Engine Model | Aircraft | Module | Stages |
|---|---|---|---|
| GE90-115B | Boeing 777-300ER, 777-200LR, 777F | LPT | 5, 6, 7 |

## Recommended Actions

1. Replace LPT Stage 5, 6, and 7 blade airfoils with GE90-upgraded trailing edge configuration P/N GE90-LPT567-TE-UPG-001.
2. Upgraded blades incorporate a 3% chord extension at the trailing edge with optimised camber distribution derived from GE90-115B-X computational aerodynamics analysis.
3. Verify blade moment-weight classification per GE CMM 72-55-00 Section 7 at installation.
4. Perform LPT rotor balance check per CMM 72-55-00 Section 12 after blade installation.

## Special Tools Required

- GE LPT module stand and blade installation fixture P/N GE90-LPT-STAND-003
- Moment-weight balancing system, resolution 0.01 g·cm
- Rotor balance machine, capacity 2,000 kg, ±0.5 g residual unbalance

## Background and Technical Details

GE Aviation issues this Service Bulletin to recommend the incorporation of an aerodynamically improved LPT Stage 5-7 blade design for GE90-115B engines, developed as part of GE's continuous improvement programme for the GE90-115B power unit. The redesigned trailing edge geometry was derived from advanced computational aerodynamics studies conducted with GE's in-house CFD tools as part of the GE90-115B-X performance improvement study, and represents a standalone improvement that can be incorporated into existing in-service engines without requiring other elements of the GE90-115B-X upgrade package.

The LPT stages 5-7 are the final power extraction stages in the GE90-115B's six-stage low-pressure turbine. The three rear stages operate at lower gas temperatures and pressures than the forward stages, and their blade geometry is primarily optimised for aerodynamic efficiency rather than thermal durability. GE's CFD analysis identified that the existing stage 5-7 trailing edge geometry produces a flow separation bubble at low-power descent conditions, corresponding to approximately 12% of the total cruise SFC penalty versus the theoretical optimum. The 3% chord extension at the trailing edge, combined with modified camber at the final 15% of chord, eliminates the separation bubble and recovers 0.09% SFC at long-range cruise conditions.

While 0.09% SFC may appear modest, for a Boeing 777-300ER operating the typical long-range international network, this translates to approximately 180 tonnes of fuel per aircraft per year at 2024 utilisation rates, or approximately USD 180,000 per aircraft at current fuel prices. GE Aviation estimates total fleet savings of USD 85 million per year if the modification is fully incorporated across the GE90-115B fleet. Operators who incorporate this SB will also receive credit under GE's Engine Efficiency Incentive Programme, which provides maintenance reserve credit for documented SFC improvements from GE-recommended modifications.

Operators planning a GE90-115B hot-section visit for the combustor dome upgrade of SB-GE-GE90-72-0312 should consider the LPT modification at the same visit where the LPT module is also due for removal.

## Definitions

- **Flow separation bubble:** A region where the boundary layer leaves the airfoil surface and recirculates, increasing loss.
- **Camber:** The curvature of an airfoil's mean line.
