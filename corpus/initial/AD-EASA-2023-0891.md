# AD-EASA-2023-0891 — LPT Stage 1 Blade Replacement

**Document Type:** Airworthiness Directive (Emergency)  
**Issuer:** European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA)  
**Effective Date:** 2023-07-14  
**Affected Engine Models:** Trent 700  
**Affected Subsystems:** LPT  
**Compliance Interval:** Before accumulating 3,500 cycles on current blade set  
**Supersedes:** None  
**Related Documents:** SB-RR-TRENT700-72-0234

## Summary

Emergency AD requiring LPT Stage 1 blade set replacement on Rolls-Royce Trent 700 engines following accelerated thermal barrier coating spallation due to a manufacturing bond coat adhesion deviation. Blade sets from the affected batch must be replaced before 3,500 cycles, and engines with blade sets above 3,200 cycles must be removed within 10 flight cycles.

|---|---|---|---|

This AD does not apply to CFM56, V2500 or PW4000 engines.

## Unsafe Condition

Accelerated spallation of the thermal barrier coating on LPT stage 1 blades exposes the blade alloy to gas temperatures that cause rapid oxidation, loss of wall thickness and, ultimately, blade failure. Multiple blade failures could cause an in-flight shutdown and damage to downstream LPT stages.

## Compliance

| Blade Set Cycles on Effective Date | Action |
|---|---|
| More than 3,200 cycles | Remove engine for module change within 10 flight cycles |
| 3,000-3,200 cycles | Replace blade set before 3,500 cycles |
| Less than 3,000 cycles, serial in affected batch | Replace blade set before 3,500 cycles |
| Records incomplete | Treat as affected if more than 3,000 cycles |

## Mandatory Actions

1. Record cycle count on installed LPT Stage 1 blades from engine historical records.
2. If blade set has accumulated more than 3,200 cycles, ground engine for module change within next 10 flight cycles.
3. Remove LPT Stage 1 blade set per RRTM TR45007-YS-1.
4. Install replacement blade set with improved oxidation-resistant coating per Rolls-Royce Approved Repair 72-55-0019 Rev 2.
5. Submit blade removal report to Rolls-Royce Engine Services within 5 business days of removal.

## Special Tools Required

- Rolls-Royce LPT Stage 1 blade removal toolset RR-T7-LPT1-RM
- Blade moment-weight balancing set per RRTM TR45007-YS-1 Section 6
- Calibrated torque wrench, range 80-350 Nm

## Safety Warnings

WARNING: LPT Stage 1 blades operate at temperatures approaching 900°C under maximum continuous thrust. Post-removal inspection of blade root shanks must be conducted under adequate lighting to detect heat damage. Blades removed under this AD must be quarantined and returned to Rolls-Royce for metallurgical analysis; they must not be re-used or sold.

## Terminating Action

Installation of a blade set with the improved EB-PVD coating per Approved Repair 72-55-0019 Rev 2 terminates the requirements of this AD for that engine.

## Background and Technical Details

EASA issues this Emergency Airworthiness Directive following a Trent 700 engine in-service event on an Airbus A330 aircraft where LPT Stage 1 blade oxidation was found to have progressed beyond the serviceable limits between scheduled overhaul visits. Post-event metallurgical analysis determined that the thermal barrier coating (TBC) on the blade suction surface had experienced accelerated spallation beginning at approximately 3,200 cycles, significantly earlier than the design life of 5,000 cycles. The root cause has been identified as a batch of blades manufactured with a bond coat adhesion specification deviation that was within tolerance at the time of manufacture but has proven sensitive to the specific thermal cycling profile of high-frequency short-haul operations.

The affected blade batch encompasses serial number ranges that Rolls-Royce has communicated to operators via Engine Status Notification ESN-TR700-2023-0147. Operators must cross-reference their blade serial number records against this notification. Where records are incomplete, all blade sets with more than 3,000 cycles must be treated as potentially affected until physical inspection of the blade TBC condition can be performed at the next engine visit.

The replacement blade set incorporates an improved EB-PVD thermal barrier coating with enhanced bond coat adhesion achieved through an optimised vacuum plasma spray (VPS) bond coat deposition process. The new coating has demonstrated TBC retention to 6,500 cycles in accelerated thermal cycling tests. Rolls-Royce has pre-positioned blade sets at major maintenance bases to minimise aircraft-on-ground time. Operators should contact their Rolls-Royce Customer Support Manager to arrange blade set availability and technical assistance.

Operators planning the module change may wish to incorporate the LPT tip seal upgrade described in SB-RR-TRENT700-72-0234 at the same visit, since the LPT module will be open and the combined work avoids a second module disassembly.

## Definitions

- **Emergency AD:** An AD issued with immediate effect because the unsafe condition requires action before the normal consultation period.
- **ESN:** Engine Status Notification, a Rolls-Royce communication to operators.
