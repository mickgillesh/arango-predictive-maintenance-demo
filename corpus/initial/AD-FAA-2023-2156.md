# AD-FAA-2023-2156 — HPT Rotor Disc Ultrasonic Inspection

**Document Type:** Airworthiness Directive  
**Issuer:** Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)  
**Effective Date:** 2023-11-08  
**Affected Engine Models:** GE90-115B, CF6-80C2  
**Affected Subsystems:** HPT  
**Compliance Interval:** At next HPT module removal, then every 2,000 cycles  
**Supersedes:** None  
**Related Documents:** SB-GE-GE90-72-0312, AD-FAA-2024-1334

## Summary

Mandatory phased array ultrasonic inspection of HPT rotor discs following identification of subsurface fatigue crack initiation in Inconel 718 disc material on GE90-115B and CF6-80C2 engines.

## Applicability

| Engine Model | Aircraft | Part |
|---|---|---|
| GE90-115B | Boeing 777-300ER, 777-200LR, 777F | HPT stage 1 and stage 2 discs |
| CF6-80C2 (all dash numbers) | Boeing 747-400, 767, Airbus A300-600, A310 | HPT stage 1 and stage 2 discs |

## Unsafe Condition

Subsurface fatigue cracks originating from non-metallic inclusions in HPT disc material can propagate to disc rupture. HPT disc rupture is an uncontained failure that can penetrate the engine case and the aircraft structure.

## Compliance

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Disc with more than 6,000 cycles since new | Inspect at next HPT module removal, not to exceed 1,000 cycles after effective date |
| All other discs | Inspect at next HPT module removal |
| After initial inspection | Every 2,000 cycles |

## Mandatory Actions

1. Remove HPT rotor disc from engine per GE CMM 72-50-00.
2. Perform phased array ultrasonic testing (PAUT) of disc bore and dovetail slots per GE NDT Procedure NDT-HPT-PA-001 Rev 4.
3. Reject disc if any indication exceeds 0.5 mm equivalent flat-bottom hole (EFBH) reflectivity.
4. Perform fluorescent penetrant inspection of dovetail slot fillets; reject if any crack indication present.
5. Verify disc bore diameter is within limits per GE Overhaul Manual Table 507.

## Special Tools Required

- GE-approved phased array UT system, 5 MHz linear array probe, minimum 64 elements
- Phased array calibration block P/N GE-HPT-CAL-001
- Fluorescent penetrant kit, Type I, Method B, sensitivity Level 3

## Safety Warnings

WARNING: HPT rotor disc is classified as a life-limited part. Any indication of cracking, no matter how small, requires immediate removal from service and notification to GE Aviation Customer Support within 48 hours. Do not attempt to re-inspect or re-polish disc surface to pass inspection.

## Reporting Requirements

Report every rejected disc to the FAA Engine Certification Office and GE Aviation within 48 hours, including disc serial number, heat code, cycles since new, and the PAUT data file.

## Background and Technical Details

This Airworthiness Directive is issued by the Federal Aviation Administration following the identification of subsurface fatigue crack initiation in HPT rotor discs of GE90-115B engines accumulating cycles on Boeing 777-300ER aircraft operated with high thrust cycles characteristic of Middle East to East Asia ultra-long-range routes. Investigation of three discs removed during scheduled overhaul revealed subsurface cracks initiating from non-metallic inclusions in the Inconel 718 disc alloy, located between 3 mm and 8 mm below the disc bore surface. These inclusions were within the original material acceptance criteria but at the boundary of acceptance, and the combination of high thermal cycling and centrifugal stress under high-thrust extended operations has resulted in earlier-than-predicted crack propagation.

The CF6-80C2, sharing a similar disc alloy specification and disc bore geometry with the GE90-115B in its HPT module architecture, has been included in this AD as a precautionary measure pending completion of fleet-wide material survey data. Current data suggests CF6-80C2 crack growth rates at the identified inclusion sizes are approximately 40% lower than GE90-115B due to lower operating thrust levels, but this margin is insufficient to extend the inspection interval beyond 2,000 cycles.

Phased array ultrasonic testing is specified as the primary inspection technique because conventional A-scan ultrasonics lacks the spatial resolution necessary to reliably detect subsurface inclusions smaller than 1.2 mm diameter. PAUT with a 64-element linear array probe provides a minimum detectable inclusion size of 0.5 mm EFBH at the relevant inspection depths. GE Aviation has established a loaner programme for operators without access to PAUT equipment; contact GE Aviation Engine Services for arrangements at least 45 days prior to required inspection.

Operators of GE90-115B engines planning a hot-section shop visit for the combustor dome upgrade in SB-GE-GE90-72-0312, and CF6-80C2 operators addressing combustor findings under AD-FAA-2024-1334, should schedule this disc inspection at the same visit where the HPT module is removed.

## Definitions

- **PAUT:** Phased array ultrasonic testing.
- **EFBH:** Equivalent flat-bottom hole — the reflectivity of a reference defect used to size ultrasonic indications.
- **Life-limited part:** A part with a mandatory replacement time in cycles.
