## Task Summary

This task gives the borescope inspection of the ten-stage V2527-A5 high pressure compressor and the in-situ measurement of the stage 6-8 labyrinth seal clearances accepted by AD-EASA-2023-1423 when the HPC module is not due for removal. It is also the standard response to an HPC trend alert from the engine condition trend monitoring programme.

## Job Set-Up

WARNING: MAKE SURE THE ENGINE CANNOT BE STARTED. PUT A "DO NOT START" TAG ON THE MASTER LEVER AND OPEN THE FADEC AND IGNITION CIRCUIT BREAKERS.

CAUTION: LET THE ENGINE COOL FOR 45 MINUTES. HOT PROBES CAN BE DAMAGED.

1. Open the fan cowls and C-ducts.
2. Install the HP rotor turning tool.
3. Calibrate the stereo measurement tip on calibration block 1R19004. The measurement error must be within ±0.1 mm, as required by AD-EASA-2023-1423.

## Procedure

### Blade Inspection, Stages 3-10

1. Remove the borescope plugs at stages 3, 4 and 5. Inspect each blade's leading edge, tip and platform while turning the HP rotor.
2. Record erosion, tip curl, nicks and cracks. On stages 5, 6 and 7, note platform erosion — it decides whether the blades can be repaired at shop visit per SB-IAE-V2500-A5-72-0103 (0.15-0.40 mm) or must be scrapped (more than 0.40 mm).
3. Continue with stages 6, 7, 8 and 10.

### In-Situ Seal Measurement, Stages 6-8

1. Insert the guide tube through the stage 6 port and position the stereo tip to view the knife edges and the seal land.
2. Turn the HP rotor to bring each of the eight measurement positions (every 45°) into view.
3. At each position, measure the knife edge height and the radial gap between knife edge tip and land.
4. Look for abradable coating loss, backing ring cracks and knife edge mushrooming.
5. Repeat for stages 7 and 8.

## Inspection Limits

| Feature | Serviceable | Reject (Remove Module) |
|---|---|---|
| Knife edge height, stages 6-8 | 1.2 mm or more | Less than 1.2 mm |
| Seal land radial clearance | Up to 1.8 mm | Greater than 1.8 mm at any position |
| Clearance asymmetry (max − min) | Up to 0.5 mm | Greater than 0.5 mm (ovality — shop investigation) |
| Backing ring crack | None | Any |
| Blade leading edge erosion, stages 3-5 | Up to 1.0 mm | Greater than 1.0 mm |
| Blade platform erosion, stages 5-7 | Up to 0.40 mm (repairable at shop) | Greater than 0.40 mm at next shop visit |
| Blade crack | None | Any |

If the measurement cannot be made with the required accuracy at any position, AD-EASA-2023-1423 requires the module to be removed for physical inspection.

## Fault Isolation Notes

| Trend Observation (AMM-GEN-77-00-00-ECTM-001) | Likely HPC Finding |
|---|---|
| T30 rising, Ps30 falling at constant N2, htBleed rising, no wash recovery | Labyrinth seal clearance growth |
| T30 and N2 rising, EGT margin falling | Blade erosion or tip clearance growth |
| Step change in N2 vibration after a stall | Blade damage — full inspection |
| T30 rising with fuel flow ratio (phi) rising | HPC and combustor — combine with AMM-V2500-72-41-00-200-801 |

In the operator's V2527-A5 fleet, the HPC is the leading driver subsystem for engines in the warning and critical risk buckets, often in combination with the combustor or the LPT. Seal clearance growth is the most common HPC finding on engines between 3,000 and 4,000 cycles since their last HPC shop visit.

## Close-Up

1. Remove the guide tube and videoscope; check the probe tip.
2. Install the borescope plugs with new seals. Torque per AMM 72-00-00.
3. Remove the HP rotor turning tool.
4. Close the C-ducts and fan cowls.

## Records

Record AD-EASA-2023-1423 compliance (in-situ method); all eight measurements per stage; the calibration result; blade findings by stage; platform erosion estimates for SB-IAE-V2500-A5-72-0103 planning; and the next due date. Retain records for the life of the HPC module.

## Background

The V2527-A5 HPC is a ten-stage compressor with variable stator vanes on the first four stages. Its rear stages work at high temperature and small blade heights, so leakage over the blade tips and through the interstage seals has a large effect on efficiency. Stage 6-8 seals see the largest thermal transients between take-off and idle, and the abradable land coating wears progressively as the knife edges rub during each cycle.

The in-situ measurement was introduced to avoid an unscheduled module removal for engines that are otherwise healthy. It is only acceptable when the measurement accuracy is demonstrated; operators should keep their stereo measurement equipment calibrated and their inspectors current. Where several measurements are close to the 1.8 mm limit, the operator should plan the shop visit rather than rely on a repeat in-situ inspection, because seal parts and the HPC interstage seal set can have long lead times.
