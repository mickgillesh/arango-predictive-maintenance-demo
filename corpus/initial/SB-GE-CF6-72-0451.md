# SB-GE-CF6-72-0451 — Fan Booster Stall Margin Enhancement and LPC Exit Guide Vane Modification

**Document Type:** Service Bulletin  
**Recommended By:** GE Aviation  
**Effective Date:** 2023-08-28  
**Affected Engine Models:** CF6-80C2  
**Affected Subsystems:** fan, LPC  
**Recommended Interval:** At next fan module removal  
**Supersedes:** None  
**Related Documents:** AD-FAA-2024-1334

## Summary

Aerodynamic improvement to the CF6-80C2 fan booster and LPC stages via redesigned exit guide vanes with 2.3° additional sweep, delivering 1.8% LPC stall margin improvement and 0.15% SFC reduction at high-altitude cruise.

## Applicability

| Engine Model | Aircraft | Module |
|---|---|---|
| CF6-80C2 (all dash numbers) | Boeing 747-400, 767; Airbus A300-600, A310 | Fan and booster (LPC) |

## Recommended Actions

1. Replace existing LPC exit guide vanes (EGVs) with redesigned EGV set P/N CF6-80-LPC-EGV-R7 which incorporates 2.3° additional sweep angle at 80% span.
2. Verify booster stage blade set is within serviceable limits per GE CMM 72-24-00 before reinstalling.
3. Perform N1 trim balance check per test procedure following module reassembly.
4. Enter modification in aircraft records as EO-CF6-72-0451.

## Special Tools Required

- GE fan module stand P/N CF6-MOD-STAND-003
- Borescope for in-situ EGV angle verification
- N1 trim balance kit per CF6-80C2 AMM 71-00-00

## Background and Technical Details

GE Aviation issues this Service Bulletin to recommend incorporation of an aerodynamic improvement to the CF6-80C2 fan booster and low-pressure compressor stages. The modification addresses a stall margin limitation identified through advanced computational fluid dynamics modelling of the LPC stage as operated in current high-altitude cruise conditions. Modern aircraft operations frequently include extended operation at FL400+ cruise altitudes, particularly on ultra-long-range routings, where LPC inlet conditions are at the margins of the original design envelope.

The redesigned LPC exit guide vane incorporates 2.3° additional sweep at 80% span, optimising the flow angle into the LPC first stage at high-altitude cruise conditions. CFD analysis predicts a 1.8% improvement in LPC stage stall margin at cruise conditions with no measurable performance penalty at takeoff or climb conditions. Rig testing on a CF6-80C2 core confirmed the predicted stall margin improvement and demonstrated an additional benefit of 0.15% reduction in SFC at long-range cruise, attributable to improved incidence angle management on the LPC stage 1 blades.

This modification is particularly recommended for operators using CF6-80C2 on ultra-long-range routings exceeding 12 hours flight time, where LPC surge protection is most critical during descent from high cruise altitudes. GE Aviation can provide a fuel savings analysis based on each operator's specific route structure and fleet size to support the business case for early incorporation. The modification has been certified under STC ST01234SE and is compatible with all dash variants of the CF6-80C2 engine.

Operators whose CF6-80C2 engines are also subject to the fuel nozzle flow testing of AD-FAA-2024-1334 may combine the work when the engine is in the shop, to reduce total downtime.

## Definitions

- **Exit guide vane (EGV):** A stationary vane that turns the flow leaving a compressor stage.
- **Sweep:** The angle by which a vane's leading edge is inclined in the flow direction.
- **STC:** Supplemental Type Certificate.
