# SB-RR-TRENT700-72-0234 — LPT Tip Seal Upgrade to Reduced-Leakage Configuration

**Document Type:** Service Bulletin  
**Recommended By:** Rolls-Royce  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-11  
**Affected Engine Models:** Trent 700  
**Affected Subsystems:** LPT  
**Recommended Interval:** At next LPT overhaul visit, not to exceed 8,000 cycles from new  
**Supersedes:** None  
**Related Documents:** AD-EASA-2023-0891

## Summary

Upgraded LPT stages 1-3 honeycomb tip seal material for Trent 700 engines, reducing seal wear rate by 40% and delivering 3.5-4.2°C EGT margin improvement, extending average time between LPT overhauls by approximately 1,500 cycles.

## Applicability

| Engine Model | Aircraft | Module | Stages |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trent 772B-60, 772C-60, 768-60 | Airbus A330 | LPT | 1, 2, 3 |

## Recommended Actions

1. Replace LPT stages 1-3 tip seals with upgraded honeycomb seal material P/N RR-LPT-SEAL-R4 at next overhaul.
2. Verify running clearances per RRTM TR45008-YS-2 after incorporating new seals.
3. Perform post-modification performance test per TR45008-YS-2 Section 8 to confirm EGT margin improvement.
4. Record modification as RR-TR700-MOD-0234 in engine history card.
5. Where the LPT module is opened for the blade replacement required by AD-EASA-2023-0891, incorporate this upgrade at the same visit.

## Special Tools Required

- RRTM LPT module stand and rotation fixture P/N RR-T7-MOD-STAND
- Digital depth micrometer for tip seal clearance measurement
- Loctite 243 thread-locking compound for seal retention screws

## Background and Technical Details

Rolls-Royce issues this Service Bulletin to recommend an upgrade to the LPT tip seal configuration on Trent 700 engines, addressing progressive tip seal wear that has been identified as the primary driver of LPT efficiency deterioration beyond 6,000 cycles. LPT tip seals control leakage from the gas path over the blade tips, and as the honeycomb abradable material wears, the increasing tip clearance reduces the work extraction efficiency of each LPT stage.

The upgraded honeycomb seal material P/N RR-LPT-SEAL-R4 uses a metallic honeycomb with an improved cell geometry that reduces wear rate by approximately 40% compared to the original design, based on 4,000-hour endurance testing in the RR Derby materials laboratory. The new material maintains its abradable properties through the full temperature cycle range of the Trent 700 LPT, including the extreme transient temperatures experienced during high power go-around manoeuvres. Post-modification rig testing on two Trent 700 cores demonstrated an EGT margin improvement of 3.5-4.2°C attributable to reduced LPT tip leakage.

The Trent 700 is a three-shaft engine, and its LPT drives only the fan. LPT efficiency therefore has a direct effect on fan speed for a given fuel flow, and seal wear shows up in trend data as a rise in turbine gas temperature with a fall in fan speed at constant fuel flow. Operators who track these parameters can use the trend to time the upgrade within the 8,000-cycle limit.

## Definitions

- **Honeycomb seal:** An abradable seal made of thin metal foil cells brazed to a backing, into which blade tip knife edges cut.
- **Three-shaft engine:** An engine with separate low, intermediate and high pressure spools.
