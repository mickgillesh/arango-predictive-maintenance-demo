# SB-IAE-V2500-A5-72-0087 — HPT Blade Thermal Barrier Coating Enhancement

**Document Type:** Service Bulletin  
**Recommended By:** IAE International Aero Engines  
**Effective Date:** 2023-10-05  
**Affected Engine Models:** V2527-A5  
**Affected Subsystems:** HPT  
**Recommended Interval:** At next HPT module removal or by 5,000 cycles  
**Supersedes:** None  
**Related Documents:** AMM-V2500-72-51-00-200-801, AMM-GEN-77-00-00-ECTM-001

## Summary

Third-generation EB-PVD thermal barrier coating upgrade for HPT Stage 1 blades on V2527-A5 engines, providing 44% improvement in TBC cycle life and addressing premature spallation on high-frequency short-haul operations. The upgraded blade set raises the blade life limit to 5,500 cycles.

## Applicability

| Engine Model | Module | Current Blade Set | Upgraded Blade Set |
|---|---|---|---|
| V2527-A5 | HPT stage 1 | 2A2xxx series, APS TBC | V2500-HPT1-BLD-UPG-003 (EB-PVD TBC) |
| V2527E-A5 | HPT stage 1 | 2A2xxx series | V2500-HPT1-BLD-UPG-003 |

## Reason

On V2527-A5 engines flying more than 10 cycles per day, the atmospheric plasma spray (APS) thermal barrier coating on HPT stage 1 blades reaches end of life at 3,800-4,200 cycles, below the certified blade limit of 5,000 cycles. Coating loss exposes the bond coat and base alloy to gas temperatures that cause oxidation and, eventually, tip and leading edge burns. The resulting unplanned HPT removals disrupt operations and increase cost.

## Recommended Actions

1. Replace existing HPT Stage 1 blades with upgraded blade set P/N V2500-HPT1-BLD-UPG-003 at next HPT module removal.
2. The upgraded blades incorporate a 7% Yttria-stabilised Zirconia (7YSZ) TBC applied by Electron Beam Physical Vapour Deposition (EB-PVD) with enhanced bond coat adhesion.
3. Update engine life records to reflect the new blade set with an upgraded life limit of 5,500 cycles.
4. Notify IAE Customer Support of blade set upgrade through PartView system.
5. Until the upgrade is incorporated, perform the HPT stage 1 borescope in AMM-V2500-72-51-00-200-801 every 1,000 cycles on engines with more than 3,000 cycles on the current blade set.
6. Do not mix upgraded and original blades in the same stage.

## Background and Technical Details

IAE International Aero Engines issues this Service Bulletin to recommend the incorporation of an enhanced thermal barrier coating on HPT Stage 1 blades of the V2527-A5 engine. The enhancement addresses premature TBC spallation observed on a subset of the fleet operating high-frequency short-haul routes with thermal cycling rates above 10 cycles per day. Under these demanding operating conditions, the existing TBC system reaches end of life at approximately 3,800-4,200 cycles against the certified limit of 5,000 cycles, creating an unplanned maintenance requirement that disrupts operator scheduling and increases maintenance costs.

The upgraded blade set P/N V2500-HPT1-BLD-UPG-003 incorporates a third-generation EB-PVD TBC with a NiCoCrAlY bond coat applied using an optimised vacuum plasma spray process. The columnar crystal structure of the EB-PVD TBC provides superior strain tolerance during thermal cycling compared to the atmospheric plasma spray (APS) TBC used in the original blade design. Accelerated thermal fatigue testing has demonstrated TBC retention to 7,200 cycles under conditions simulating maximum-rate thermal cycling, representing a minimum 44% improvement over the original design.

The economic case depends on the operation. For operators averaging fewer than 6 cycles per day, the original coating usually lasts to the certified limit and the upgrade can be deferred to the next scheduled HPT removal. For high-cycle operators the upgrade avoids one unscheduled HPT removal per engine over a typical 10-year period, worth approximately USD 1.1 million per event in labour, parts and lost aircraft availability.

Coating loss is detectable through trend monitoring before it causes damage. As the TBC spalls, more cooling air is needed to hold blade metal temperature; on the V2527-A5 this appears as a gradual rise in HPT cooling bleed (W31) and a rise in EGT at constant T30. Operators should use the HPT signature in AMM-GEN-77-00-00-ECTM-001 to prioritise borescope inspections and upgrade timing.

## Engine Health Monitoring Indicators

| Indicator | Sensor / Station | Signature of HPT Coating Loss |
|---|---|---|
| HPT coolant bleed | W31 | Gradual rise of 2% or more |
| EGT | T50 / EGT margin | Rising at constant T30 |
| HPC exit temperature | T30 | Stable (distinguishes HPT from HPC) |
| Core speed | Nc | Small increase for the same thrust |

## Definitions

- **TBC:** Thermal barrier coating — a ceramic layer that insulates the blade metal from the gas.
- **EB-PVD:** Electron beam physical vapour deposition — a coating process producing a columnar, strain-tolerant ceramic layer.
- **APS:** Atmospheric plasma spray — an older coating process producing a lamellar ceramic layer.
- **Spallation:** Loss of coating in flakes.
