## Task Summary

This task gives the borescope inspection of the V2527-A5 combustor — the segmented liner, the bulkhead and the 20 airspray fuel injectors — and the procedure for removing, flow testing and cleaning fuel injectors. It is the accomplishment procedure for the V2527-A5 inspection required by AD-FAA-2024-0583.

## Job Set-Up

WARNING: CLOSE THE ENGINE LP FUEL VALVE AND TAG IT BEFORE YOU REMOVE ANY FUEL INJECTOR. RESIDUAL FUEL UNDER PRESSURE CAN CAUSE INJURY AND FIRE.

WARNING: DISCONNECT THE IGNITION LEADS AND WAIT 5 MINUTES FOR THE EXCITER TO DISCHARGE.

CAUTION: LET THE ENGINE COOL FOR 90 MINUTES AFTER SHUTDOWN.

1. Open the fan cowls and the C-ducts.
2. Remove the igniter plugs and the combustor borescope port plugs B1 to B3.
3. Install the HP rotor turning tool on the gearbox.

## Procedure

### Borescope Inspection

1. Insert the videoscope through the left igniter port. Inspect the outer liner segments from the bulkhead to the liner exit.
2. Inspect the injector tips visible from this port. Turn the HP rotor slowly and use the HPT nozzle guide vanes as a position reference.
3. Repeat from the right igniter port and from ports B1 to B3 to cover the full circumference and the inner liner.
4. For each liner segment, record cracks (length, location, depth estimate), burn marks, coating loss and segment distortion.
5. For each injector, record carbon deposit coverage of the air swirler and fuel spray orifice.
6. Inspect the HPT stage 1 nozzle guide vanes for hot streaks. If burn or erosion is found, do AMM-V2500-72-51-00-200-801.

### Fuel Injector Removal, Flow Test and Cleaning

1. Remove the fuel injectors that are at reject limits, or the full set where cycles since cleaning exceed 1,500.
2. Flow test each injector on fixture IAE-FN-TEST-003 at three flow points (start, idle, take-off). Record flow and spray cone angle.
3. Reject injectors with flow variation exceeding ±4% from nominal at any point, or with a spray pattern asymmetry visible on the patternator.
4. Clean serviceable injectors in carbon solvent OMat 150 per the soak schedule; re-test.
5. Install the injectors with new seals 2A4417. Torque per AMM 73-11-41.
6. Do a fuel leak check at idle.

## Inspection Limits

| Feature | Serviceable | Re-inspect in 50 Cycles | Reject |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liner segment crack | Up to 12 mm | 12-25 mm | Greater than 25 mm, or more than 50% through thickness |
| Segment distortion (lip curl) | Up to 1.0 mm | 1.0-2.0 mm | Greater than 2.0 mm |
| Bulkhead heat shield crack | Up to 15 mm | Not applicable | Greater than 15 mm or missing material |
| Injector swirler blockage | Up to 10% | 10-25% | Greater than 25% |
| Injector flow variation | Within ±4% | Not applicable | Outside ±4% |
| Injector cycles since cleaning | Up to 1,500 | Not applicable | Greater than 1,500 |

## Fault Isolation Notes

| Trend Observation (AMM-GEN-77-00-00-ECTM-001) | Likely Finding | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Fuel flow ratio (phi) rising, T30 and P30 stable | Injector coking | Flow test and clean injectors |
| EGT spread widening | Blocked injector or distorted segment | Locate hot streak; replace injector |
| phi rising together with HPC drift (T30, Nc) | Combined HPC and combustor deterioration | Combine with AMM-V2500-72-00-00-200-802 |
| HPT coolant bleed (W31) rising with EGT | Liner distress disturbing HPT cooling | Inspect HPT nozzle guide vanes |

V2527-A5 engines flagged with HPC and combustor as joint driver subsystems are common in the fleet. The two inspections should be planned together and parts such as the combustor liner and the fuel nozzle set (20x) checked for availability before the engine is opened, because both are long-lead items when not in stock.

## Close-Up

1. Install the borescope port plugs and igniter plugs with new gaskets.
2. Connect the ignition leads; open the LP fuel valve; remove the tags.
3. Remove the HP rotor turning tool.
4. Close the C-ducts and fan cowls.
5. Do an engine ground run at idle; check for fuel leaks and confirm EGT spread.

## Records

Record AD-FAA-2024-0583 compliance; liner findings by segment; injector condition by position; flow test results; cleaning or replacement; and the EGT spread before and after.

## Background

The V2527-A5 combustor uses a segmented liner: individual cast nickel-alloy segments, each with its own cooling features, are bolted to a support shell. This design lets a damaged segment be replaced without replacing the whole liner, but each segment edge and each attachment is a potential crack initiation site. The 20 airspray fuel injectors mix fuel with HPC air in a swirler before it enters the combustor.

In high-cycle short-haul operation, V2527-A5 combustors see the same low-cycle thermal fatigue that affects other annular combustors. Coked injectors make it worse by creating hot streaks. AD-FAA-2024-0583 requires the liner and injectors to be inspected together. Operators that keep injector cleaning at 400 cycles and monitor EGT spread can apply for the 350-cycle interval.
